Frequently Asked Questions

Funding Questions, Simplified.

General information about our core funding areas.

What is CGTMSE?

CGTMSE is a credit guarantee framework. Actual eligibility, coverage and lender terms depend on applicable guidelines and the lending institution.

Can every business get government funding?

No. Each scheme has its own eligibility, purpose, documentation and application requirements.

What is working capital?

Working capital supports the operating cash cycle of a business, including receivables, inventory and supplier obligations.

What documents may be needed?

Depending on the case, KYC, business registrations, GST, ITR, financial statements, banking information and other documents may be requested.

Does MONEY4YOU guarantee approval?

No. Funding, guarantees, subsidies and scheme benefits are subject to eligibility, documentation, applicable rules and lender or authority approval.

How do I start?

Use the Funding Assessment form and provide your business requirement, approximate amount and purpose.
