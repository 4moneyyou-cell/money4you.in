Frequently Asked Questions

Funding Questions, Simplified.

Common questions about CGTMSE, government funding, working capital and the MONEY4YOU process.

What is CGTMSE?

CGTMSE refers to a credit guarantee framework for eligible credit facilities. Specific eligibility, coverage and terms depend on applicable guidelines and lender assessment.

Can every business receive government funding?

No. Government schemes have specific eligibility criteria, business categories, purposes, documentation and approval processes.

Can MONEY4YOU guarantee loan approval?

No. Funding decisions are made by the relevant lender or authority based on eligibility, documents, financials and applicable policies.

What documents may be required?

Depending on the case, documents may include KYC, business registrations, GST records, ITRs, financial statements, bank statements, ownership information and funding-purpose documents.

What is working capital funding?

It is funding used to support operating requirements such as inventory, receivables and day-to-day business cash cycles.

How do I start?

Submit the enquiry form with your business details, approximate funding amount and purpose. We can then discuss the next steps.
