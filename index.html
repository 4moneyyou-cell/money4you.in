FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do you guarantee loan approval?

No. Funding decisions are subject to eligibility, documentation, lender policies and final credit assessment.

Can you help MSMEs explore CGTMSE-backed funding?

We can provide guidance for eligible businesses exploring CGTMSE-related funding routes, subject to applicable rules and lender assessment.

What information should I keep ready?

Basic business details, turnover, banking information, existing obligations, funding purpose and relevant financial documents are useful for an initial assessment.

Is the calculator an approval tool?

No. It is only an indicative planning tool to help you communicate your requirement.
